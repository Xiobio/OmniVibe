Create a cinematic premium software-product key visual in a landscape 16:9 composition. RIGHT HALF: one large front-facing sculpted dark obsidian command core carrying the reference's exact central terminal chevron and underscore and NINE separated annular jade segments, with one champagne-gold segment. Around it, a restrained spatial arrangement of nine small dark translucent equipment-like panels connected by very fine jade traces, showing only abstract tiny lines and status dots, no readable text. Materials: jade glass, satin black ceramic, brushed warm gold. Soft green reflections on an obsidian surface, subtle architectural depth, precise and elegant, game-inventory atmosphere. LEFT HALF: calm almost-black jade gradient with subtle haze, plenty of clean negative space for later typography, no extra object. No text, logos beyond the supplied symbol, letters, watermarks, humans, computers or keyboards. No game franchise imagery. Avoid chaotic neon, rainbow, high-density particles or a gaming poster with characters. This is branded conceptual artwork, not a fake screenshot. High production polish.
